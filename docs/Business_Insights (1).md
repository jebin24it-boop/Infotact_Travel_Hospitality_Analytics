## **Business Insights**

This document presents the key business insights derived from the Hotel Reservation Analysis Dashboard developed using Power BI. These insights help understand booking trends, customer behavior, cancellation patterns, and pricing strategies. 

## **1\. High Cancellation Rate**

The dashboard shows that the cancellation rate is high. Out of total bookings, a large number of bookings were cancelled. This indicates that the hotel should focus on reducing cancellations by analyzing factors such as lead time, market segment, room type, and meal plan.

**Business Recommendation:**  
The hotel can reduce cancellations by offering confirmation reminders, flexible booking policies, and special discounts for early confirmations.

## **2\. Online Segment Has More Bookings**

The market segment analysis shows that the online segment has the highest number of bookings compared to other segments. This means that online platforms are the main source of hotel reservations.

**Business Recommendation:**  
The hotel should continue improving its online presence through booking websites, digital marketing, and attractive online offers.

## **3\. Average Lead Time is High**

The average lead time is high, meaning many customers book their rooms several days before their arrival date. Long lead time bookings may also have a higher chance of cancellation.

**Business Recommendation:**  
The hotel can send reminder emails or messages before the arrival date to confirm the booking and reduce cancellation risk.

## **4\. Repeat Guest Rate is Low**

The dashboard shows that the repeat guest rate is low. This means most customers are new guests, and only a small percentage of customers return for another booking.

**Business Recommendation:**  
The hotel can introduce loyalty programs, reward points, membership discounts, and personalized offers to increase repeat bookings.

## **Conclusion**

The dashboard helps identify important business patterns in hotel reservations. The key insights show that cancellations are high, online bookings dominate, lead time is high, and repeat guest rate is low. These findings can help the hotel improve customer retention, reduce cancellations, and increase overall booking performance.
